The Mosok is a semi-automatic rifle in NT;RE. Each shot deals 43.1 damage to the body, at up to 360 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 29 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 43.06 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.
