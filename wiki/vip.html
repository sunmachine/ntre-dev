In Extract or Kill the VIP, one team escorts a lightly armed VIP to its extraction zone while the other team tries to kill them. VIP is also the name of the special class the escorted player becomes. The mode uses the same capture zones as CTG.

Winning a round

  • VIP escort: the VIP (class NEO_CLASS_VIP) enters the radius of an active capture zone owned by their team (or a neutral zone) while the round is live.[12][13]
  • VIP killed: the team opposing the escorting team wins (when sv_neo_vip_ctg_on_death=0).[14]
  • VIP disconnects (m_pVIP becomes null): the opposing team wins by forfeit (when sv_neo_vip_ctg_on_death=0).[15]
  • Team elimination, same as CTG.[16]
  • Time runs out: stalemate.[17]

Who becomes the VIP

The escorting team is chosen at random in the first round and then alternates. The VIP is a random player on that team who has cl_neo_vip_eligible 1 (the default). If nobody has, it's anyone on the team.[18] At the start of the next round the VIP goes back to their previous class.[19]

The VIP class

  • 100 HP with Assault movement speeds (160 run, 256 sprint).
  • No cloak and no vision mode.
  • Can't pick up a knife, and can't change class while VIP.[20][21]
  • Spawns with a MilSO. The primary loadout starts with the SMAC (one 90-round magazine, no reserve) and unlocks more with rank:

With sv_neo_vip_ctg_on_death 1, the VIP's death doesn't end the round. "HVT DOWN" is announced, a ghost spawns where the VIP fell, and the round continues as a ghost capture.[28]

Maps None of the official maps has a _vip suffix, and VIP is left out of the default random rotation. The mode is supported in code, but no official map is built for it.
