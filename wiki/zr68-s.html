The ZR68-S is a suppressed fully automatic rifle in NT;RE. Each shot deals 32.7 damage to the body, at up to 566 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 22 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 32.67 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.
