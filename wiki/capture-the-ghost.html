Capture the Ghost

CTG, NT;RE's core competitive mode

Capture the Ghost (CTG) is NT;RE's core mode. Each round, an AI core called the ghost spawns somewhere on the map. A team wins the round by carrying it into its capture zone or by wiping out the enemy team. You get one life per round.

Winning a round

  • Ghost capture: a live player carrying the ghost enters the radius of an active capture zone owned by their team (or a neutral zone) while the round is live. The round is won instantly; there is no hold timer.[3][4]
  • Team elimination: the enemy team has 0 living members. If both teams reach 0 at once, the round is a stalemate.[5]
  • Time runs out with no capture or elimination: stalemate ('TIE'). Neither team gets a round win and nobody gets round-end XP.[6]
  • Map logic can force a winner through neo_game_config FireTeamWin / FireRoundTie inputs.[7]
Time-out is always a draw When the clock runs out it's a stalemate, no matter who holds the ghost or how many players are alive. The code has a "win by numbers" outcome, but nothing triggers it.[8]

Capture zones

Capture zones (neo_ghost_retrieval_point) belong to one team or are neutral. The capture registers the instant a living ghost carrier is within the zone's radius; there's no hold timer. Zones are checked 10 times a second, using 3D distance from the zone's centre.[15] Zone ownership swaps every round along with the spawns.[16]

Overtime

Off by default (sv_neo_ctg_ghost_overtime_enabled 0). When it's on and the ghost is being carried with 10 s or less left, the round can run up to 45 s past the limit while the ghost stays in hand. If the carrier drops it, the time left shrinks back to the 10-second grace.[17]

XP and rewards

A ghost capture is the most valuable thing you can do in NT;RE. Every surviving player on the capturing team is raised straight to the next rank threshold (0 → 4 → 10 → 20).[18] Dead teammates get the normal +1.

Winning by elimination gives +1 to the whole team, +1 more to each survivor, and +1 more to the ghost carrier. A surviving carrier therefore gets +3.[19] See Loadouts & ranks.

Cap prevention

If the last living player on a team kills themselves, disconnects or dies to their own team's grenade while the enemy is carrying the ghost, the enemy team still gets the capture reward when it wins the round (sv_neo_suicide_prevent_cap_punish 1).[20]
