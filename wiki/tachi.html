The Tachi is a selective-fire sidearm in NT;RE. Each shot deals 25.2 damage to the body, at up to 495 rounds per minute. Every Assault spawns with one.

Damage

Base damage 17 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 25.25 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.
