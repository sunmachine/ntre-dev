Game modes

Team Deathmatch

TDM: respawning team combat scored by kills

Team Deathmatch (TDM) is a respawn mode. Two teams fight for one long round, and whichever team has the higher score when time runs out wins. The official map is ntre_bullet_tdm.

Contents
  1. Winning
  2. Rules
  3. Scoring

Winning

  • When time runs out, the team with the higher team score wins the round.[1]
  • Equal team scores at time-out: stalemate.[2]
  • Match ends when a team reaches the score limit in rounds won (default 1, so the first round won ends the match).[3]
One round The default score limit is 1 round won, so a TDM match is a single 10.25-minute round.[4]

You respawn 10 seconds after death and get the loadout menu again.[6] For the first 5 seconds after spawning you take no damage, unless you fire or drop a weapon first.[10] Within 10 seconds of spawning you can still change class.[11]
