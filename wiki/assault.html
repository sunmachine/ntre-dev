The Assault is the middle class. It has more health than Recon, a short cloak, motion vision that picks out anything that moves, and the largest primary weapon selection in the game, from SMGs up to the SRS sniper rifle and AA13 auto-shotgun.

  1. Strengths and weaknesses
  2. Motion vision
  3. Loadout

Strengths and weaknesses

  • Biggest sprint multiplier. Sprint is ×1.6 of run speed (160 → 256 u/s), against Recon's ×1.35. Recon still sprints faster in absolute terms (270).[14]
  • Sprint costs AUX. 5 AUX per second while moving, so a full bar lasts 20 seconds. AUX does not regenerate while sprinting, and every time you stop sprinting another 0.5 AUX is taken.[15]
  • Short cloak. 8 seconds, recharging at 0.25/s, so 32 s from empty to full.[16]
  • Spawns with a grenade, along with the Tachi pistol and knife.[13]
  • 1.15-second slowdown after landing a jump, like every class except Recon.

Motion vision

Any model moving faster than 0.5 u/s is drawn with an extra highlight pass, and that includes cloaked players. A cloaked enemy who stands perfectly still disappears again.[17] See Vision modes.
