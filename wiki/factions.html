Factions

Jinrai and the NSF

NT;RE matches are fought between two teams: Jinrai and the NSF. They are mechanically identical. No health, speed, AUX, cloak or vision value differs between them. The differences are cosmetic and positional.

Each class has three skins per team.[5] The VIP and Juggernaut each use one model shared by both teams.[6]

Spawn sides and capture zones swap every round, so neither team keeps a map-side advantage.
