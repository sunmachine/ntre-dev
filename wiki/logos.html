Branding

Logos

The marks and images this wiki uses, where each came from, and where it appears

The wiki uses four brand images. The NT;RE mark from Wireframe is the primary logo and appears in the header of every page. The other three come from the NEOTOKYO;REBUILD project's Open Collective page. Each asset is shown below on a dark and a light background.

NT;RE mark

RolePrimary logo: the site header, beside a WIKI label
Fileimg/nt-mark.svg, inlined into each page by build.py
SourceWireframe, crates/viewer/web/src/app/header/glyphs.ts at 3e7a535. It is drawn from the terminal art tools/logo.sh prints, with each character a 1×2 cell.
ColoursLetters in the current text colour, drop shadow in the same colour at 30%, dot #d1482e
Rights© 2026 Dan Peavey. All rights reserved (Wireframe's licence).

The letters inherit the surrounding text colour, so the mark follows the theme with no second file: light on dark pages, dark on light ones. The dot stays the same red in both.

Favicon

The browser-tab icon, img/favicon.png: the NT; badge scaled to 64×64. The disc keeps it readable on both light and dark tab bars.

Open questions

  • Three reds. The Wireframe mark uses #d1482e, the badge #c62a1d, and the wordmark #ff0000, which the site also uses for its dots. Choosing one would make the wiki consistent. See Colours & type.
  • Favicon. It still comes from the Open Collective badge. It could switch to Wireframe's app icon so the tab matches the header.
