Style guide

How this wiki is written and laid out, in short form

The rules every page on this wiki follows. The guide is part of the prototype's source and changes in the same commit as the style it describes, so it always matches the pages around it.

Contents
  1. Voice
  2. Facts and sources
  3. Numbers and units
  4. Names
  5. Page anatomy
  6. Work-in-progress modes
  7. Visual

Voice

  • Plain and direct. Address the player as you, in the present tense.
  • Open with what the thing is and why it matters. Name the subject in bold in the first sentence.
  • One idea per sentence. Cut filler, hype and hedging that carries no information.
  • British spelling (colour, armour, behaviour). Code identifiers and quoted strings keep their own spelling.
  • Headings in sentence case: Weapon speed scale, not Weapon Speed Scale.

Facts and sources

  • Every gameplay number cites the line it comes from: [[src:path:line]] becomes a footnote linked to GitHub at the pinned commit.
  • Never guess. If the code is ambiguous, or client and server disagree, say so in an amber callout labelled Unconfirmed, Unverified or Unresolved.
  • Say how a claim was established: "inferred from the code" is different from "tested in game".
  • Tables and infoboxes take their numbers from the extracted data, not from hand-typed copies.

Names

  • Weapons use their in-game display names: MPN45, SRM-S, ZR68-L, Murata Supa-7, MilSO, BALC.
  • Classes are capitalised: Recon, Assault, Support, VIP, Juggernaut.
  • Game modes take the full name first, then the code: Capture the Ghost (CTG).
  • Teams: Jinrai and NSF. The objective is the ghost, lowercase in running text.
  • Console variables and file names go in code, with their default where it matters: mp_friendlyfire 1.

Page anatomy

  1. Category, title, and a one-line subtitle
  2. An infobox for anything with stats: weapons, classes, modes
  3. A lead paragraph that stands on its own
  4. Sections under h2 headings. A contents list appears automatically from three sections up.
  5. A navbox linking related pages, then the generated references
  • Comparisons go in tables, not lists. Numbers are right-aligned in monospace.
  • Link a page's first mention in each section, not every mention.
  • Page addresses are flat and lowercase: capture-the-ghost.html.
  • Callouts have a short bold label. Neutral callouts are for notes; amber ones are for anything quirky or unverified.

Work-in-progress modes

  • VIP and Juggernaut are still in development. Stat lists keep them, but last, greyed out and tagged WIP. This includes their weapons, the SMAC and BALC.
  • Headline counts leave them out: the main page says 3 classes and 3 game modes, with a note naming the two in development.
  • Their own pages, and their weapons' pages, open with an amber Work in progress callout.

Visual

  • Dark, minimal, neutral. Hairline borders; no shadows, gradients or emoji.
  • Red is for the brand dot only: after page titles and beside the current page. Never for link text, because red text means a missing page.
  • Link underlines are a cool, desaturated cyan. On hover the line fills left to right, snaps into place, pulses once and settles. Sidebar items and cards get the same motion as a hairline along their bottom edge.
  • The NT;RE mark is the primary logo. The wordmark appears only on the main page.
  • Image slots are grey placeholders labelled with what belongs there, until assets are licensed.
