Loadouts & ranks

How XP, rank and class decide which weapons you can pick

You earn XP in NT;RE by killing enemies and winning rounds, and you lose it for teamkills and suicides. Your XP sets your rank, and your rank unlocks primary weapons for your class. XP can go negative, which drops you to Rankless Dog.

A capture reward (ghost capture or VIP escape) lifts each surviving player on the winning team to the next rank threshold, so a capture is worth far more than a kill. A Lieutenant gets +1 instead.[16] Servers can replace this with a flat amount using sv_neo_cap_reward, or extend it to dead players with sv_neo_cap_reward_dead.

XP doesn't change while the game is paused.[17]

Changing loadout

After spawning you have 25 seconds (sv_neo_time_alive_until_cant_change_loadout) to change loadout. The window closes early if you fire or drop a weapon. During idle, warmup and countdown there is no limit.[24]
