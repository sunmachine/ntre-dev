The AA13 is a fully automatic shotgun in NT;RE. Each shot deals 185.6 damage to the body if all 5 pellets hit, at up to 180 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 25 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 37.12 per pellet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.

Shots to kill assume every pellet hits. Damage = script damage × 1.485 (sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier) × hit-location multiplier.

Technical notes

  • Full-auto shotgun (NEO_WEP_AA13 in IsAutomatic list).
  • No 'Penetration' key; pellets fired with FireBulletsInfo_t default penetration 0 [5][20], so no penetration.
  • Constant 5-degree spread (min == max) and AA13 PrimaryAttack does not add accuracy penalty [21].
  • AA13 checks curtime - m_flLastAttackTime < CycleTime but never updates m_flLastAttackTime; effective rate limit is m_flNextPrimaryAttack (+CycleTime per shot, line 112). RPM assumed = 60/CycleTime.
