Branding

Colours & type

The palette and typefaces of the wiki's dark theme

The palette follows the NEOTOKYO;REBUILD branding: neutral black, white and a red dot, plus one cool accent for link underlines. Red is kept to small marks. It never colours link text, because on a wiki red text means a page that doesn't exist yet.

A light palette exists in the stylesheet behind data-theme="light", ready for a theme switch. Without one, every page renders dark whatever the OS setting.

Marks

  • Red dot after page titles, raised like the dot in the wordmark
  • Red dot beside the current page in the sidebar
  • Links in white with a faint desaturated-cyan underline. On hover it fills left to right, snaps, pulses once and settles
  • Hairline borders, no shadows or gradients
