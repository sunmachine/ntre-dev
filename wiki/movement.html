Movement

Speeds, sprinting, jumping, leaning and fall damage

Movement speed in NT;RE is built up from several multipliers: your class, the weapon in your hands, your stance, and whether you are sprinting or aiming. All speeds are in Hammer units per second (u/s). The base is 200[1] u/s, which is Recon's run speed.

Speed formula

Each tick, the game recomputes your maximum speed in this order:[2]

  1. Start from the class run speed: Recon 200, everyone else 160.
  2. Multiply by the active weapon's speed scale (0.675–1.0, see the table below).
  3. Crouching: ×0.75. Walking: ×0.75. The two stack, so a crouch-walk is ×0.5625.
  4. Sprinting: ×1.35 for Recon, ×1.6 for Assault, VIP and Juggernaut. Support can't sprint.
  5. Aiming down sights: ×0.6.
  6. Never below 55 u/s.
  7. For 1.15 s after a jump, every class except Recon is slowed further. The penalty is strongest on landing and fades over that time, with a floor of 75 u/s.

Moving backwards on the ground is scaled by a further ×0.75.[3]

Sprinting

Sprinting drains AUX at 5 per second for every class except Recon, and only while you are actually moving.[65] You need at least 2 AUX to start sprinting.[66] You can't aim while sprinting, and you can't sprint while carrying the ghost.[67][68]

Jumping

The default jump height is 36 units. Recon jumps 1.5× higher (54 u) and the Juggernaut 1.4× (50.4 u).[69] There is no NEO-specific crouch-jump bonus; crouch-jumping follows standard Source behaviour.[70] Recon's super jump adds a horizontal boost.

Air control

In the air, any speed above your normal run speed bleeds away. With the default sv_neo_airfriction 0 nothing is lost, but objective players (the ghost carrier, VIP and Juggernaut) use sv_neo_airfriction_objective 0.5, which stops them from bunny-hopping the objective across the map.[71]

Leaning

You can lean while alive, on the ground and not sprinting. Pressing both lean keys cancels the lean.[72] Leans are asymmetric: 7.5 units to the left and 15 to the right, to match the original NEOTOKYO. A full lean takes 0.2 s, rolls the camera 20° and lowers your eyes by 2 units.[73] Leans are shortened automatically when there's no room.

Footsteps

Footsteps start at 50 u/s. While walking or aiming, you make no footstep sounds as long as you stay at or below 70% of your class run speed (or of your crouch speed when crouched).[74]

Fall damage

Falls faster than 580 u/s hurt. Damage is (speed − 580) × 100 / 444 × 0.25, about 0.056 HP per u/s over the limit.[75][76]

Note Because of the 0.25 scale (sv_neo_falldmg_scale), a fall at Source's nominal "fatal" speed of 1024 u/s does only about 25 damage. Damage is linear and uncapped above that.
