    Jitte-S¶

    Overview¶

    The Jitte-S is a suppressed fully automatic submachine gun. It's in the loadout of Recon (from Corporal).

    General stats¶

    DamageCycle timeRPMDPSMagazinePenetrationMove speed
    22.27120.0909 s36603245304205×0.86
    Damage is per bullet to the head or chest, with the ×1.485 modifier applied. DPS is damage ÷ cycle time: the most it can do, with every shot landing.

    Damage¶

    No headshot bonus

    A headshot deals the same damage as a shot to the chest.

    Each bullet does 22.27 damage to the head or chest, and less to the stomach, arms and legs. Damage doesn't drop off with distance.

    Hit locationMultiplierDamage
    Head7×122.27
    Chest8×122.27
    Stomach9×0.7516.71
    Arms10×0.7516.71
    Legs11×0.613.36

    Its penetration is 20.5 See how penetration works for what that gets through.

    Technical details

    Damage is the weapon's base damage of 15, × the global bullet modifier of 1.485 (sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier)2, × the hit-location multiplier.

    Handling¶

    Your shots spread out as you keep firing, move or jump, and tighten again when you stop. Aiming keeps them tighter.12 See accuracy for how fast it changes.

    SpreadMin coneMax cone
    Hip fire3°7°
    Aimed1°3°
    Technical details
    • View kick per shot: pitch -0.5–-0.25 degrees, yaw -0.6–0.6 degrees.
    • Aimed field of view: 38 degrees13 (lower is more zoom).

    Can be picked up by: All classes except Juggernaut15.

    Suppressor¶

    No muzzle flash, and firing it doesn't make your cloak flash. Bots also have a harder time hearing it.16

    Technical notes
    • weapon_bits.h calls it 'Jitte (with scope)' 17; script printname is just 'Jitte'.
    • Its GetNeoWepBits() returns NEO_WEP_JITTE | NEO_WEP_SUPPRESSED, not NEO_WEP_JITTE_S 18. So it is SUPPRESSED, uses the Jitte handlingTable row (12; the JITTE_S row at :132-135 has identical values), and is NOT flagged SCOPEDWEAPON (no scope overlay).
