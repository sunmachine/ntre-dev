Weapons

Every weapon in NT;RE, compared

NT;RE has 29 weapons and items. Your class and rank decide which primary you can pick. Each class always spawns with its own sidearm, knife and grenade or detpack.

Two things to know Every bullet's damage is multiplied by 1.485 (sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier), and the numbers below already include it. There is no headshot bonus: head and chest both take ×1.0. See Damage & penetration.

Suppressed weapons

Suppressed variants (marked S) don't show a muzzle flash and don't flash your cloak when you fire. In exchange they usually lose damage, penetration or rate of fire compared with the unsuppressed version: the SRM-S fires at 660 RPM against the SRM's 990, and the MX-S has penetration 18 against the MX's 38.
