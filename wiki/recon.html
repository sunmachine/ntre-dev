Light scout class: fastest, longest cloak, most fragile

The Recon is NT;RE's scout. It has the highest base speed, the longest thermoptic camouflage, a sprint that costs no AUX, and a super jump. In exchange it has the lowest health of the three selectable classes and the narrowest choice of primary weapons.

Strengths and weaknesses

  • Fastest class. Runs at 200[2] u/s and sprints at 270[3][4] u/s, against 160/256 for Assault.
  • Free sprint. Sprinting does not drain AUX, and AUX keeps recharging at 2.5/s while you sprint.[15] AUX is only spent on the super jump.
  • No landing penalty. Every other class slows down for 1.15 s after a jump. Recon doesn't.[16]
  • Highest jump: 54 units, 1.5× the default.[17]
  • Fragile. 100 HP, the joint lowest with the VIP.

Super jump

Hold Sprint and press Jump while moving forward or backward. It adds a horizontal boost equal to your current maximum speed on top of a normal jump, and costs 45 AUX.[18][19]

The jump only fires if all of these are true:[20]

  • You are sprinting, on the ground, and not standing in water above your feet
  • You are not carrying the ghost
  • You have at least 45 AUX
Quirk The code takes the 45 AUX whenever those conditions pass, but only applies the boost if exactly one of forward or back is held. A sprint-jump with only strafe keys held costs 45 AUX and gives no boost. Holding a strafe key alongside forward cuts the boost to ×0.707.[21] This is inferred from the code and has not been confirmed in play.

The boost follows your view direction with the vertical part removed but not renormalised, so looking up or down shrinks it by the cosine of your pitch. Super jump at eye level for maximum distance.[22]

Thermoptic camouflage

Recon has a 13-second cloak that recharges at 0.55 per second (about 23.6 s from empty to full), against Assault's 8 s at 0.25/s.[23] See Thermoptic camouflage for what reveals a cloaked player.

Night vision

Recon uses night vision, a full-screen brightening filter. It doesn't highlight anything specific, unlike motion or thermal vision.

Recon has the smallest hull: 64 units standing and 46 crouched, with eyes at 57 units (Support: 70 / 59 / 60).[25][26]
