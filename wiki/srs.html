The SRS is a semi-automatic marksman rifle in NT;RE. Each shot deals 148.5 damage to the body, at up to 43 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 100 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 148.5 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.

Bolt action

After a shot, a bolt (ACT_VM_PULLBACK) animation is queued 0.08 s after firing once attack is released; cannot re-fire until CycleTime has passed since the last shot. Bolting can be cancelled by quick-switching; reload skips the separate bolt.
