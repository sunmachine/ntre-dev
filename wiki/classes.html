Classes

The three selectable classes, plus the two special roles assigned by game modes

Every player in NT;RE picks one of three classes: Recon, Assault or Support. The class sets your health, movement speed, vision mode, whether you can cloak, and which primary weapons you can choose from at each rank. Two more roles, VIP and Juggernaut, can't be picked from the menu. Their game modes assign them.

The two teams, Jinrai and NSF, have no gameplay differences. They differ only in player models, HUD colour and which spawn they start from.

Fast scout with the longest cloak, a super jump and free sprinting. Fragile.

100 HP · 270 sprint · 13 s camo

Assault

The all-rounder. Motion vision, a short cloak and the widest weapon selection.

120 HP · 256 sprint · 8 s camo

Support

Heavy with almost twice the health of anyone else, thermal vision and machine guns. Cannot sprint or cloak.

225 HP · no sprint · thermal

Choosing and changing class

Class changes apply immediately while you are dead, during the pre-round freeze (before you fire or drop a weapon), during warmup, or in DM/TDM within 10 seconds of spawning. At any other time the change is queued for your next spawn.[31]

Servers can cap or ban classes with sv_neo_class_limit_recon, sv_neo_class_limit_assault and sv_neo_class_limit_support (default -1, unlimited; 0 bans the class). If the class you ask for is full, you get the first class that isn't.[32][33]

Shared rules

  • No armour. Health is the only durability stat; no class has damage reduction.[34]
  • Speed depends on the weapon in hand. Every weapon multiplies your class speed by 0.675–1.0. See Movement.
  • A knife backstab kills any class. It deals exactly 226 damage, one more than Support's maximum health.[35]
  • No prone. Stand, crouch and lean are the only stances.
