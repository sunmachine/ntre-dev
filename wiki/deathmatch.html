Game modes

Deathmatch

DM: free-for-all, first to 50 XP

Deathmatch (DM) is free-for-all. Every other player is an enemy, there are no teamkill penalties, and the first player to 50 XP wins the map.

Contents
  1. Winning
  2. Rules
  3. Spawns
  4. Scoring

Winning

  • A single player reaches sv_neo_dm_win_xp (50) XP or more while the round is live, and no other player is tied with them.[1]
  • At time-out the single highest-XP player wins. If several players are tied, the round goes to Overtime and continues until one player leads alone.[2]
  • When a winner is decided the game goes to intermission (map end).[3]

Teamplay is off, so there's no reflected team damage and every kill counts as an enemy kill.[4] Every player's XP resets to 0 at the start of each round.[12]

Spawns

If the map has a saved spawn file (maps/dm_locs/<map>.loc), you spawn at a random unused position from it. Otherwise spawn points are shuffled every time.[13][14]
