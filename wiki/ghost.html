The Ghost

The objective item in Capture the Ghost

Ghost
SlotPrimary
Carried byAny class except Juggernaut

The ghost is the objective in Capture the Ghost. Whoever carries it can see every enemy within about 46 metres, but everyone else on the map can see where the carrier is.

Contents

  2. Enemy beacons
  3. Being the carrier

Picking it up

  • It goes in the primary slot. You can't pick it up while holding a primary weapon, so drop yours first.[4]
  • It can't shoot or aim.[5]
  • Picking it up stops your sprint, and the Recon super jump is disabled while you carry it.[6][7]
  • The Juggernaut can't carry it.[8]
Unconfirmed Whether a carrier can sprint again after picking it up is unclear. The client blocks it, but the server's check doesn't mention the ghost. This needs testing in game.

Enemy beacons

While the ghost is your active weapon, it boots up for 3.3 seconds and then shows red beacons with distance labels on every living enemy within 45.72 m (1800 units), through walls.[9] Your teammates don't see them; spectators watching you do.[10]

The ghost also beeps faster as the nearest enemy gets closer: every 0.1 × distance in metres seconds, between 1 and 3.5 s.[11]

Being the carrier

Everyone else sees a pulsing marker on the ghost or its carrier: red to the enemy, your team colour to friendlies.[12] You also get the objective air friction: no bunny-hopping the ghost across the map.[13]

Spawning and dropping

  • One ghost spawns each round at a random ghost spawn point.[14] With sv_neo_ghost_spawn_bias (on in competitive), rounds play in pairs from the same spawn, so each team gets the same start once.[15]
  • It drops when the carrier dies or disconnects, or when they press drop while holding it. A dropped ghost is never removed.[16][17]
  • If it's thrown or carried into an out-of-bounds trigger, it's sent back to where the last carrier last stood on solid ground.[18]
