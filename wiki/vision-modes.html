Vision modes

Night, motion and thermal vision

Every class except the VIP has a toggleable vision mode. Each class gets a different one. Vision modes cost no AUX, have no time limit, and switch off when you die or respawn.[1] You can toggle at most once every 0.1 s.[2]

ModeClassesHighlightsReveals cloak?
Night visionRecon, JuggernautNothing specific: brightens the whole sceneNo
Motion visionAssaultAny moving modelYes, while the target moves

Night vision

Used by the Recon and Juggernaut. It is a full-screen filter only: no entity is drawn differently.[3]

Motion vision

Used by the Assault. Along with the screen filter, any model whose speed is above 0.5 u/s is drawn again with a highlight material. That includes players, physics props and cloaked players.[4] A target that stands completely still isn't highlighted.

Thermal vision

Used by the Support. Uncloaked players, NPCs, ragdolls and gibs are drawn as hot. The smoke screen overlay is switched off, so Support can see through smoke.[5][6]

Cloaked players are not drawn hot. They appear with their normal material, which makes thermal a poor counter to thermoptic camouflage.[7]
