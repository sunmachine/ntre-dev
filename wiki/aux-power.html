AUX power

The energy bar behind sprinting and the Recon super jump

AUX power
Maximum100[1]
Sprint drain5[2] /s
Min. to sprint2[3][4]
Sprint-stop cost0.5[5]
Regen delay0.5[6] s
Super jump45[7]

AUX power is the energy bar on your HUD. In NT;RE it powers only two things: sprinting and the Recon super jump. Thermoptic camouflage has its own separate battery, and vision modes are free.[8][9]

Sprint costs

  • Sprinting drains 5 AUX per second, but only while you are actually moving horizontally. Holding sprint while standing still costs nothing.[12]
  • Each time a sprint ends, an extra 0.5 AUX is taken. Tapping sprint on and off is more expensive than holding it.[5]
  • Recon is exempt from both: its sprint drains nothing and costs nothing to stop.
Unverified For Recon, ending a sprint doesn't restart the 0.5 s regen delay, because the code returns early before updating the timer.[13] Whether this makes a visible difference in play hasn't been confirmed.

Console variables

  • sv_infinite_aux_power (default 0, cheat) disables AUX drain.[14]
