The Support is the heavy class. With 225 HP it can take almost twice the punishment of an Assault. It sees uncloaked players as hot silhouettes through thermal vision, even through smoke, and is the only class that can carry the PZ252 machine gun. It cannot sprint and cannot cloak.

  3. Loadout

Strengths and weaknesses

  • Highest health of any selectable class: 225.[11] There is no armour on top of it. Only a knife backstab (226 damage) is guaranteed to kill outright.[12]
  • Cannot sprint. A Support sprint speed constant (216) exists in the code but is commented as reserved for future use.[13][14]
  • No AUX use at all, and no thermoptic camouflage.
  • Thermal vision sees through smoke. The smoke screen overlay is switched off while thermal is active.[15]
  • Biggest target: a 70-unit standing hull, the largest of the selectable classes.

Thermal vision and cloak

Thermal vision draws uncloaked players, NPCs and corpses as hot. A cloaked player is drawn with their normal material and no thermal highlight, so they don't stand out from the cold background the way you might expect.[16]

Loadout
