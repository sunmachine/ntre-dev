Damage & penetration

How bullet damage, hit locations, wall penetration, accuracy and explosions work

Damage in NT;RE is simple: script damage × a global modifier × a hit-location multiplier, reduced only when the bullet passes through a surface. There's no armour, no headshot bonus and no falloff over distance.

Damage formula

damage = weapon damage × 1.485 × hit-location multiplier [× penetration loss][1][2]

  • 1.485 is sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier, applied to every bullet. Explosives use sv_neo_exp_dmg_modifier, also 1.485.[3]
  • No range falloff. A bullet does the same damage at any distance until it hits something.[4]
  • Fractional damage carries over. Only whole HP is removed per hit, but the leftover fraction builds up per attacker and is applied once it reaches 1.[5]
  • No armour. Classes differ only in maximum health: Recon 100, Assault 120, Support 225.[6]

No headshot bonus NT;RE sets the head multiplier to 1.0, where Half-Life 2 uses 2.0. A headshot does exactly as much damage as a chest shot.

With sv_neo_hitboxgroup_pen 1 (default 0), a shot that hits an arm or leg is traced again to see whether it would also have hit the head, chest or stomach behind it, and uses that multiplier instead.[12]

Penetration

Every gun has a penetration value. When a bullet hits a surface, the game measures how thick it is (up to 12 units) and divides that by the material's value. If the result is no more than the bullet's remaining penetration, the bullet goes through.[13]

  • Damage after the wall: damage × max(0.25, 1 − used ÷ penetration). A bullet keeps at least 25% of its damage.[14]
  • The penetration used is subtracted, so the bullet can carry on through further walls with what's left.[15]
  • Bullets never pass through players.[16]
  • Shotgun pellets (buckshot) have penetration 0 and never go through anything. The Supa-7 slug does.
Naming trap The code calls this table PENETRATION_RESISTANCE, but the values work as a divisor: a higher number means the surface is easier to shoot through. Dirt and sand (0.2) are the hardest; glass (0.9) is easy.

Accuracy

Each weapon has a minimum and maximum spread cone, one pair for hip fire and one for aiming. The current cone sits between them according to an accuracy penalty from 0 to 1.[38]

EffectPenalty
Each shot fired+0.8[39]
Airborne, per second+3.0[40]
Moving without aiming (speed > 5), per second+2.0[41]
Recovery, per second−1.2[42]
Extra recovery while crouched, per second−1.0[43]
 The penalty is capped at 1.0. One shot takes the penalty most of the way to maximum spread, so tap-firing and crouching matter.

The SRS, ZR68-L and BALC also add up to 1° of extra vertical recoil after 10 consecutive shots. This comes from reading the code and hasn't been tested in play.[44]

Explosions

Explosive damage falls off linearly from full at the centre to zero at the edge of the radius.[45]

  • Frag grenade: 270 × 1.485 = 401 damage, 270-unit radius
  • Detpack: 300 × 1.485 = 445 damage, 350-unit radius
  • BALC charged shot: 270 damage, 256-unit radius (the explosive modifier is not applied)

Team damage

Friendly fire is on by default (mp_friendlyfire 1).[46] For the first 7 seconds of a live round, damage you deal to a teammate is reflected back to you at ×2 and your teammate takes none. If the reflected damage kills you, you can't respawn that round.[47][48]
