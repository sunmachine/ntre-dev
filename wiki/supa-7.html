The Murata Supa-7 is a semi-automatic shotgun in NT;RE. Each shot deals 213.8 damage to the body if all 8 pellets hit, at up to 75 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 18 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 26.73 per pellet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.

Shots to kill assume every pellet hits. Damage = script damage × 1.485 (sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier) × hit-location multiplier.

Technical notes

  • No 'Penetration' key in script; buckshot pellets are fired with FireBulletsInfo_t penetration 0 [5][30], so pellets do not penetrate. Only the slug penetrates (32).
  • Shotguns are excluded from the shot-manipulator recoil [31].
  • Supa7 PrimaryAttack does not add accuracy penalty per shot; spread only grows from movement/airborne [32][33].
  • GetMinBurst/GetMaxBurst (1/3) in weapon_supa7.h:43-44 are NPC AI hooks, not a player fire mode.
  • Script key 'clip_size2' (line 24) is not the parsed key name ('clip2_size', [34]), so it is ignored.
