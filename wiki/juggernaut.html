JGR mode and the JGR56 juggernaut suit

In Juggernaut (JGR), both teams fight over a single JGR56 suit. The player who activates it becomes the Juggernaut: 990 HP, a BALC heat weapon and night vision. While their team has the suit, its kills score points. First team to 20 points wins the round.

Winning a round

  • A team reaches sv_neo_jgr_max_points (20) team points while the round is live.[12]
  • Time-out with no living juggernaut, and nobody on the trailing team is mid-activation: the higher team score wins.[13]
  • Time-out with equal scores and no juggernaut: stalemate.[14]
  • Time-out while a juggernaut is alive, or while the trailing team is activating the suit: Overtime. Overtime ends when the juggernaut's team is ahead (they win), or when the juggernaut is gone and the first condition applies.[15]

The suit

  • Spawns at a juggernaut spawn point each round, and stays locked until 20 seconds after the freeze ends ("JUGGERNAUT ENABLED").[16]
  • To activate it, hold Use within 150 units for 5 seconds. You're frozen while you hold, and letting go, dying or moving away cancels it.[17]
  • When you activate it you drop all your weapons, get full health and 100 AUX, and dead teammates are respawned immediately.[18]
  • When the Juggernaut dies, the suit drops where they fell and can be taken again.[19]

Playing as the Juggernaut

  • 990 HP, no bleeding, can't be gibbed.[20]
  • Only weapon: the BALC, which has no magazine. It overheats after 50 shots, locks for 5 s, and has a charged grenade alt-fire. It doesn't slow you down (×1.0).
  • Can't pick up the ghost or any other weapon.
  • Your position is always shown to everyone on the compass.[21]
  • Jumps 1.4× higher than normal (50.4 u). AUX regenerates at 10/s.
Unresolved Juggernaut sprint speed: the per-tick speed code gives 256 u/s, but the defined constant and GetSprintSpeed() say 270. 256 is probably what players get. Not yet checked in game.

Respawning

JGR has respawns (10 s delay), but the Juggernaut's team can't respawn while the Juggernaut is alive, or for 8 seconds after they die.[22]
