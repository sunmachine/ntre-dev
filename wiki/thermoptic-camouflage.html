Thermoptic camouflage

The active cloak used by Recon and Assault

Thermoptic camouflage (TOC) makes the wearer near-invisible. Recon and Assault have it; Support, the VIP and the Juggernaut don't.[6] It runs on its own battery, separate from AUX.

Battery

The cloak drains 1 unit per second, applied in whole-second steps, and switches off when the battery drops to 0.1.[2] It only recharges while switched off.

What gives you away

The cloak isn't perfect. Ranked roughly from most to least revealing:

  • Motion vision. Assault motion vision highlights any model moving faster than 0.5 u/s, cloaked or not.[8]
  • Moving. Each cloaked player has a visibility factor from 0 to 1 that controls how strong the shimmer and tint are. Standing still (≤20 u/s) lowers it by 1.1 per second; moving raises it by speed × 0.004 per second, so about +0.8/s at a full 200 u/s run.[9][10]
  • Switching it on. Activation emits a blue light flash (radius 96 units, 0.5 s) and a sound that nearby players can hear.[11]
  • Firing an unsuppressed weapon causes a 0.1 s flash. Suppressed weapons don't.[12]

Bots get a bonus to spotting you for a moment after you activate the cloak or fire an unsuppressed weapon.[13]

Cloaked players and their weapons cast no shadows.[14]

Against thermal vision

Support thermal vision draws uncloaked players as hot. A cloaked player is drawn with their normal material and no thermal highlight.[15]

What doesn't break it

Only these turn the cloak off: running out of battery, toggling it yourself, dying, or respawning.[16] No code switches it off when you fire, take damage or pick up the ghost.

Console variables

  • sv_neo_infinite_cloak (cheat) disables battery drain.[17]
