The Knife is an melee weapon in NT;RE. Every Recon, Assault, Support spawns with one.

Contents
  1. Attacks
  2. Availability
  3. Technical notes

Attacks

A slash deals 25 damage[3] at up to 51 units. A backstab deals 226[4]: one more than Support's 225 HP, so it kills any selectable class outright. The global damage modifier does not apply to the knife.

Availability

Spawns with: Recon[8], Assault[9], Support[10].

Can be picked up by: All classes except VIP and Juggernaut[2].

Technical notes

  • sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier is NOT applied (it is only applied in ModifyFireBulletsDamage on bullets, [11]).
  • AMBIGUOUS: knife damage goes through DispatchTraceAttack [12], so hitgroup multipliers likely apply to both normal and backstab hits, but the hitgroup reported by the knife's line/hull trace was not verified.
  • No secondary attack [13].
