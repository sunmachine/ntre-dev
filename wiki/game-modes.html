Game modes

Game modes

The seven game types and the round structure they share

NT;RE has seven game types. Capture the Ghost is the core competitive mode, and 24 of the official maps are built for it. Alongside it are the respawn modes TDM and DM. Juggernaut and VIP are objective modes still in development.

Round times include the 15-second pre-round freeze. VIP and Juggernaut are in development.

Round flow

All the scored modes follow the same cycle:[18]

  1. Warmup (45 s): runs once when a match starts. Players respawn freely and XP doesn't count.[19]
  2. Pre-round freeze (15 s): you can look around and change loadout but can't move, and you can't be hurt.[20]
  3. Live round. The round timer includes the freeze, so a 3.25-minute CTG round has about 3:00 of live play.[21]
  4. Overtime, where the mode has it.
  5. Post-round (15 s, mp_chattime), then the next freeze.[22]

When the match leaves warmup, team scores, rounds won and every player's XP reset to 0.[23] Spawn sides and capture zones swap between the teams every round.[24]

A round won't start while either team is empty; it retries every 10 seconds.[25] In one-life modes you can still spawn up to 15 s after the freeze ends (sv_neo_latespawn_max_time).[26]

Match length

  • The "score limit" counts rounds won, not kills.[27]
  • With a round limit set, the match ends early once the trailing team can no longer catch up.[28]
  • If rounds won are tied when the limit is reached, play continues in sudden death until a team wins a round (neo_round_sudden_death 1).[29]

Choosing the mode

By default each map sets its own mode through a neo_game_config entity. A map without one falls back to Free Roam.[30] Servers can override this with sv_neo_gamemode_enforcement:

ValueBehaviour
0Mode set by the map (default)
1Always sv_neo_gamemode_single[31]
2Random from the sv_neo_gamemode_random_allow bitmask, without repeats until every allowed mode has been played. The default mask, 11, covers TDM, CTG and DM.[32]
3Player vote, using each client's neo_vote_game_mode[33]

Competitive features

CTG, VIP and JGR support the competitive extras. sv_neo_comp 1 turns them all on at once: a ready-up lobby (5 players per team by default), .pause/.unpause (60 s or 180 s), spectators limited to their own team, and paired ghost spawns.[34]

Other modes

  • Training (TUT): map-scripted tutorials (ntre_class_tut, ntre_shooting_tut) with no round logic. Completing them earns achievements.[35]
  • Free Roam (EMT): no objective and no scoring. You can change team, class and loadout while alive.[17]

Bots

Bots have dedicated behaviour for CTG (seeking, carrying and escorting the ghost, lone-wolf ambushes) and JGR. In TDM, DM and VIP they fall back to generic seek-and-destroy. You can command bots with Use.[36][37]
