Getting started

The rules that matter most in your first matches

NT;RE plays differently from most shooters. These are the mechanics that decide fights, each with a link to the full detail.

1. You get one life per round

In Capture the Ghost, the main mode, you don't respawn until the next round. A round is about 3 minutes of play after a 15-second freeze. Rounds end on a ghost capture or when one team is wiped out. If time runs out it's a draw, whoever holds the ghost.

2. Headshots aren't special

Head and chest both take ×1.0 damage; stomach and arms ×0.75; legs ×0.6. Aim at centre mass. There's no damage falloff over distance, and every bullet does ×1.485 its listed base damage. See Damage & penetration.

3. Your class decides how you fight

4. XP unlocks weapons

Kills (+1), assists (+1) and round wins (+1) raise your rank, which unlocks better primaries. Teamkills and suicides cost 1 XP. A ghost capture jumps every surviving teammate straight to the next rank, which is worth far more than a few kills. XP resets at the start of every match. See Loadouts & ranks.

5. Watch what's in your hands

Your movement speed depends on the weapon you're holding, from ×1.0 down to ×0.675 for the PZ252. Switch to your sidearm or knife (×0.85) to rotate faster. See Movement.

6. Cloak isn't invisibility

Thermoptic camo is hard to see when you're still, but Assault motion vision highlights you the moment you move. Firing an unsuppressed weapon makes you flash, and so does switching the cloak on. Suppressed weapons keep you hidden.

7. Don't shoot teammates early

Friendly fire is on. During the first 7 seconds of each round, damage to teammates is reflected back to you at double strength. See Team damage.
