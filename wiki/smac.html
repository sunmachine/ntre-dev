The SMAC is a fully automatic submachine gun in NT;RE. Each shot deals 17.8 damage to the body, at up to 660 rounds per minute.

Damage

Base damage 12 × the global bullet modifier of 1.485[4] gives 17.82 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no headshot bonus and no damage falloff over range.
