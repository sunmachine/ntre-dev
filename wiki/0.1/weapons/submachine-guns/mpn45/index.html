    MPN45¶

    Overview¶

    The MPN45 is a suppressed fully automatic submachine gun. It's in the loadout of Recon (from Any (incl. Rankless Dog)), Assault (from Any (incl. Rankless Dog)) and Support (from Any (incl. Rankless Dog)).

    General stats¶

    DamageCycle timeRPMDPSMagazinePenetrationMove speed
    17.82120.0758 s37923235304125×0.856
    Damage is per bullet to the head or chest, with the ×1.485 modifier applied. DPS = damage ÷ cycle time, a theoretical ceiling.

    Damage¶

    No headshot bonus

    A headshot deals the same damage as a shot to the chest.

    Base damage 12 × the global bullet modifier of 1.4852 gives 17.82 per bullet to the head or chest. NT;RE has no damage falloff over range.

    Penetration 125: see Damage & penetration for how far that reaches through walls.

    Handling¶

    Spread grows from the minimum to the maximum cone as you fire, move or jump, and recovers when you stop.12 See accuracy for the rates.

    SpreadMin coneMax cone
    Hip fire4°7°
    Aimed1°4°
    • Camera recoil: pitch -0.5 to -0.25, yaw -0.6 to 0.6, scaled ×1 from the hip and ×1 aimed.
    • Aimed field of view: 50 degrees13 (lower is more zoom).

    Suppressor¶

    Suppressed weapons have no muzzle flash, don't trigger the cloak flash when fired while cloaked, and are harder for bots to hear.19
