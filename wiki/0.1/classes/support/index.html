    Support is the heavy class. With 225 HP it can take almost twice the punishment of an Assault. It can't sprint and can't cloak.

    Thermal vision shows uncloaked players as hot silhouettes, even through smoke. Support is also the only class that can carry the PZ252 machine gun.

    Strengths and weaknesses¶

    • Most health. 225, the most of any class you can pick, with no armour on top.11 Only a knife backstab (226 damage) is guaranteed to kill you outright.12
    • Sees through smoke. Smoke isn't drawn while thermal vision is on.13
    • Can't sprint.1415
    • No AUX and no cloak. Support doesn't use AUX at all, and has no thermoptic camouflage.
    • Biggest target. Standing, Support is 70 units tall, the largest of the classes you can pick.
    Technical details

    The code has a Support sprint speed of 216, but it's commented as reserved for future use.1415

    Thermal vision and cloak¶

    Thermal vision shows uncloaked players, NPCs and corpses as hot. Cloaked players don't glow. They look normal, with no heat highlight, so they don't stand out against the cold background the way you might expect.16

    Loadout¶

    You always spawn with a knife, the Kyla revolver and a smoke grenade.10
