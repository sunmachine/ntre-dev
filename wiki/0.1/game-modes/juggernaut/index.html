    Work in progress

    Juggernaut is still in development. Its rules and numbers are likely to change.

    Not a class you can pick

    The Juggernaut is a role the Juggernaut mode assigns. You can't choose it from the class menu, and it only exists in this mode.

    In Juggernaut (JGR), both teams fight over a single JGR56 suit. Whoever activates it becomes the Juggernaut, with 990 HP, a BALC heat weapon and night vision.

    While a team has the suit, its kills score points. The first team to 20 points wins the round.

    Winning a round¶

    • A team reaches 20 team points while the round is live.12
    • Time-out with no living juggernaut, and nobody on the trailing team is mid-activation: the higher team score wins.13
    • Time-out with equal scores and no juggernaut: stalemate.14
    • Time-out while a juggernaut is alive, or while the trailing team is activating the suit: Overtime. Overtime ends when the juggernaut's team is ahead (they win), or when the juggernaut is gone and the first condition applies.15
    Technical details

    As extracted from the code:

    • A team reaches sv_neo_jgr_max_points (20) team points while the round is live.12

    The suit¶

    • It spawns at a juggernaut spawn point each round. It stays locked until 20 seconds after the freeze ends ("JUGGERNAUT ENABLED").16
    • To activate it, stand within 150 units and hold Use for 5 seconds. You're frozen while you hold. Letting go, dying or moving away cancels it.17
    • When you activate it, you drop all your weapons and get full health and 100 AUX. Dead teammates respawn immediately.18
    • When the Juggernaut dies, the suit drops where they fell and can be taken again.19

    Playing as the Juggernaut¶

    • 990 HP. You don't bleed and can't be gibbed.20
    • Your only weapon is the BALC. It has no magazine. It overheats after 50 shots and locks for 5 seconds, and has a charged grenade alt-fire. It doesn't slow you down (×1.0).
    • You can't pick up the ghost or any other weapon.
    • Everyone can always see your position on the compass.21
    • You jump 1.4 times as high as normal, to 50.4 units.22
    • One player model, shared by both teams.23
    • AUX regenerates at 10 per second.

    Unresolved: sprint speed

    The Juggernaut probably sprints at 256 units per second, but the code disagrees with itself. The movement code gives 256. The defined constant and GetSprintSpeed() say 270. This hasn't been checked in game yet.

    BALC¶

    The BALC is the Juggernaut's heat-cooled machine gun, and the only weapon the suit can use.

    General stats¶

    Damage¶

    No headshot bonus

    A headshot deals the same damage as a shot to the chest.

    Each bullet does 53.46 damage to the head or chest, and less to the stomach, arms and legs. Damage doesn't drop off with distance.

    Technical details

    Damage is the weapon's base damage of 36, × the global bullet modifier of 1.485 (sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier)25, × the hit-location multiplier.

    Handling¶

    Your shots spread out as you keep firing, move or jump, and tighten again when you stop. Aiming keeps them tighter.34 See accuracy for how fast it changes.

    SpreadMin coneMax cone
    Hip fire2°6°
    Aimed1°3°
    Technical details
    • View kick per shot: pitch 0.25–0.5 degrees, yaw -0.6–0.6 degrees.
    • Camera recoil: pitch -0.25 to -0.75, yaw -0.6 to 0.6, scaled ×1 from the hip and ×0 aimed.
    • Aimed field of view: 45 degrees35 (lower is more zoom).

    Heat system¶

    There's no magazine. Each shot uses up part of a 5036-shot heat reserve, which refills by itself while the gun isn't overheated. Run it dry and the gun overheats, locking up for 5 seconds37.

    PropertyValue
    Capacity5036
    Cooling interval (s)0.3538
    Underwater cooling interval (s)0.139
    Overheat lockout (s)537
    Mode (s)witch time (s)0.240
    Technical details

    No clip (clip_size -1). The 50 'default_clip' rounds act as a heat/energy pool: each shot consumes 1; at 0 the gun overheats and locks for 5 s, then refills to 50. While not overheated, 1 round regenerates every 0.35 s (0.1 s when submerged). Not reloadable, cannot be dropped.

    Charged grenade¶

    In its alternate fire mode, hold fire for 1 second41 to charge it, then fire a bouncing grenade that does blast damage.

    In alt mode, hold attack for 1 s to charge, then fire a bouncing grenade_ar2 projectile (2000 u/s) that does radius damage.

    Unconfirmed: extra recoil on long bursts

    The BALC adds up to 1 degree of extra vertical recoil after 10 shots in a row. This comes from reading the code and hasn't been tested in play.47

    Technical notes
    • Juggernaut-only weapon, given by GiveDefaultItems 48; not a loadout choice.
    • No NSF viewmodel key, so both teams use v_balc.mdl (fallback at 49).
    • Effective damage computed with the bullet modifier assumes primary-mode bullets go through the normal FireBullets path (BaseClass::PrimaryAttack, 50).

    Respawning¶

    JGR has respawns, after a 10-second delay. But the Juggernaut's team can't respawn while the Juggernaut is alive, or for 8 seconds after they die.51
