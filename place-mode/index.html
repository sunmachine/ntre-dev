Place mode, as it might look

A mock-up of the Nav and Place control beside BASE, HEAD and DIFF. HEAD is the real saitama nav. BASE is made up for the illustration: an older nav where a third of Spark is not stamped yet, Fan Spawn is still called Fans, and two areas beside AC belong to it. Drag to pan, scroll or use the buttons to zoom. The keys are 1, 2 and 3 for the sides, Z for Nav and Place, and K for labels.

Spark is being stamped

In HEAD, Spark covers 46 areas as one patch with one outline. BASE lacked 16 of them, so in DIFF they wear the added hatch in Spark's own colour.

Fans became Fan Spawn

A hue comes from the name, so a rename changes the colour between BASE and HEAD. In DIFF the area wears the changed dots.

Two areas left AC

BASE had them as AC and HEAD has no place for them. In DIFF they keep AC's colour and wear the removed hatch, outside AC's outline.

The same changes in Nav

Today's colouring sees each of these as a changed area, amber with dots, and cannot say which place moved.

HEAD is ntre_saitama_ctg from the asset repository on 2026-10-27, 1348 areas and 39 places, drawn top down with lower floors first. Floors that overlap in plan hide one another here; the viewer draws them in 3D. Each label sits at the centre of its place's bounds, as the developer ruled, so a place in two pieces gets one label between them.
