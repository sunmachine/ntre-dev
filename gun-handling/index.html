Bullet spread

Each bullet lands at random inside a cone around where the camera points. This is the only recoil the bench shows for the MX on master. With it on, the dots are bullets and the line is the camera. With the camera climb on as well, a shaded band along the camera's path shows where bullets can land.
How wide the cone is for a rested gun, in degrees across.
How wide the cone is at its peak in held fire, in degrees across.
How far each shot moves the cone from its first shot width toward its held fire width. 1 is the whole way in one shot. The game stores this value for each gun.
How fast the cone closes again, all the time, firing or not. 1 closes the whole way in one second. How much comes off between two shots depends on shots per second, under Burst. The game stores this value for each gun.

Camera climb

The camera moves with each shot and stays there. Off, the camera never moves, as on the MX on master, and the random, recovery and climb path levers have nothing to act on. The MPN on master has it on, with a random climb and no climb path.
The least the camera can rise on one shot, in degrees. Each shot draws its rise at random between this and the most. The game stores this value for each gun.
The most the camera can rise on one shot, in degrees. The game stores this value for each gun.
How far the camera can move left or right on one shot, in degrees. Each shot draws its drift at random between this much left and this much right. The game stores the left limit and the right limit separately for each gun, and the bench uses one value for both.
How much of the random range is applied to the climb.
0 is a flat draw. 1 pulls draws toward the centre.
Share of the climb the camera gives back by itself after the burst.

Climb path

The fixed path the camera follows as it climbs. It is data stored with the gun, and the levers below apply to whichever one is chosen. Off is a gun with no climb path, which is every gun on master.
Chance that a burst follows the mirror image of the climb path. 0 never flips, 0.5 flips half the bursts and 1 always flips.
How much of the fixed, learnable climb is applied.
Makes single shots of the climb path kick harder or softer at random. The average climb stays the same.

Burst

How fast the gun fires. A faster gun leaves the spread less time to close between two shots. On master the MX fires 9.4 shots a second and the MPN 13.2.
Shots in one burst.
How many bursts stay on the plot. The newest is drawn solid and the earlier ones are faded.
